## 5. Tone of voice

**Is:** precise · calm · concrete · quietly confident · specific about numbers and named practices.
**Is not:** hype, superlatives, emoji, exclamation marks, "passionate", "ninja", "rockstar", "I love coding".

**Rules**
1. Prefer a number to an adjective. "~50 business modules" beats "large-scale".
2. Name the practice, not the buzzword. "Human-in-the-loop validation" beats "AI-powered".
3. Never claim what the audit doesn't support — every website string traces to `PROFILE.md`.
4. Short declarative sentences. Let the substance carry the weight.
5. Qualify honestly where it strengthens credibility (e.g. how LOC was counted).

## 6. Visual direction

**Concept — "Structured depth."** The work is about imposing order on complexity: layered architecture, enforced boundaries, systems that stay legible at scale. The visual language should read as engineered rather than decorated — precise grids, deliberate depth, restrained motion. Financial-software seriousness, contemporary product craft.

**Colour** — deep near-black ground (not pure black) with a cool blue-violet cast; a single saturated accent used sparingly for emphasis and interaction; a secondary cyan-teal for the 3D scene and data accents. Gradients are atmospheric — used for depth and light, never as decoration on their own. Full token set in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

**Typography** — editorial contrast. A tight geometric or grotesque sans for oversized display headlines; a highly legible neutral sans for body; a monospace for code, metrics, labels and technical annotation. Monospace carries the engineering signal — use it for numbers and tags, not for paragraphs. All three faces must include **Armenian and Cyrillic** coverage.

**Motion** — motion clarifies structure. Reveals stagger along the reading order; transitions imply spatial continuity; hover states respond within 150ms. No animation without a job. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` path that removes movement, never content.

**3D — the chosen concept: a layered architecture field.** A slowly rotating three-dimensional lattice of connected nodes arranged in *discrete horizontal strata*, each stratum a Feature-Sliced Design layer — app, pages, widgets, features, entities, shared. Connections flow downward between layers and never sideways across them, which is literally the architectural rule his work enforces. Cursor movement parallaxes the field and lights nearby nodes.

This is chosen over particles, neural networks and abstract geometry because it is the only option that *means something specific about him*: it is a picture of the discipline he actually practises. Mobile renders a simplified two-layer version at reduced node count; reduced-motion renders a single static frame.

**Content style** — evidence first. Every technology shown answers: what I use it for, what it solves, how deeply, where, and why it matters. No logo walls without context.
